INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT

Lead Setter & On-Call Job-Assist Services

This Independent Contractor Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of _______________, 2026 ("Effective Date"), by and between:

Company: Easy Garage Cleaning, owned and operated by Zac Bezenek
Fort Collins, Colorado
("Company")

Contractor: Tyler _______________
Address: _______________
Phone: _______________
Email: _______________
("Contractor")

  1. 1. Scope of Services

    Contractor provides two categories of service under this Agreement:

    A. Lead Setting. Contractor responds to inbound customer inquiries (calls, texts, form submissions, and messages) on behalf of the Company; qualifies prospective customers and gathers job details (address, scope, timeline); schedules and books appointments for garage cleanouts, junk removal, and related services; follows Company-provided scripts, pricing guidelines, and customer communication standards; and maintains accurate records of all leads contacted, response times, and appointments booked.

    B. On-Call Job Assists. From time to time, the Company may offer Contractor the opportunity to assist on-site with a specific job (e.g., hauling, loading, cleanout labor). Each job assist is a discrete, project-based engagement. Contractor may accept or decline any job-assist request at Contractor's sole discretion, with no penalty for declining. Acceptance of one request does not obligate Contractor to accept future requests.

  2. 2. Compensation

    A. Lead-Setting Commission.

    • $30.00 per booked appointment — A "booked appointment" is defined as a confirmed, scheduled service appointment with a qualified customer that appears on the Company's calendar.
    • $3.00 per rapid response — Paid for each unique customer inquiry that Contractor responds to within two (2) minutes of the inquiry being received, regardless of whether the inquiry converts to a booked appointment.

    B. Job-Assist Rate.

    • $20.00 per hour for each on-call job assist that Contractor elects to accept. Hours are measured from arrival at the job site to completion of the assigned project scope. Contractor logs hours; Company confirms against job records.

    C. Payment Terms.

    Payment for all compensation earned during a given week (Monday through Sunday) shall be issued by the following Friday. If Company revenue from completed jobs has not yet settled by Friday, Company may extend payment by up to two (2) business days (i.e., the following Tuesday). This grace period applies only when the delay is caused by revenue settlement timing; it does not excuse withholding for any other reason. Company will notify Contractor by Friday if the grace period is being used.

    Company will provide Contractor with a weekly earnings summary generated from Quo (the Company's lead-tracking and earnings platform). Contractor has five (5) business days after receiving the summary to dispute any discrepancies.

    As an independent contractor, Contractor is responsible for all federal, state, and local taxes on earnings. Company will issue a 1099-NEC form for tax year 2026 if total payments equal or exceed $600.

  3. 3. Independent Contractor Status

    Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner, or agent of the Company. Contractor shall:

    • Control the manner and means by which lead-setting services are performed.
    • Set their own schedule for lead-setting work, provided response-time targets are met.
    • Provide their own phone, computer, and internet access for lead-setting work.
    • Have the unrestricted right to decline any on-call job-assist request without consequence.
    • Not be entitled to employee benefits, workers' compensation, unemployment insurance, or any other employee benefits from the Company.

    Job assists are project-based engagements with a defined scope and completion point. Company may provide tools and materials for on-site work, but this does not alter Contractor's independent status. Contractor is not required to maintain set hours, accept a minimum number of job assists, or be exclusively available to the Company.

  4. 4. Term and Termination

    This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues on an at-will basis. Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, by providing seven (7) days written notice (text or email is acceptable). Upon termination:

    • Company shall pay Contractor for all commissions, rapid-response bonuses, and job-assist hours earned through the termination date, on the next regular payment cycle.
    • Contractor shall return or delete any Company materials, customer lists, login credentials, and proprietary information within 48 hours.

  5. 5. Confidentiality

    Contractor agrees to keep confidential all non-public business information, including but not limited to: customer lists, contact information, pricing structures, marketing strategies, scripts, and internal processes. This obligation survives termination of this Agreement for a period of two (2) years.

  6. 6. Non-Solicitation

    Note: This clause is subject to Colorado Revised Statutes § 8-2-113 and related restrictions on restrictive covenants. Both parties acknowledge this provision should be reviewed by legal counsel before reliance. If any portion is found unenforceable under Colorado law, the remainder of this Agreement remains in full effect.

    During the term of this Agreement and for a period of six (6) months following termination, Contractor agrees not to directly solicit customers whose contact information Contractor obtained solely through work performed under this Agreement, for the purpose of providing substantially similar services (garage cleanouts, junk removal, or hauling). This restriction applies only to customers Contractor personally interacted with during the final six (6) months of the engagement.

  7. 7. Performance Standards & Tracking

    To qualify for the rapid-response bonus, Contractor must respond within two (2) minutes of the inquiry timestamp as recorded by Quo, the Company's lead-tracking and earnings platform. Quo's system records shall be the sole authority for determining response times, booked-appointment counts, and commission calculations. Contractor has access to Quo to review their own metrics at any time.

  8. 8. Use of Company Brand

    When communicating with customers, Contractor shall represent themselves as acting on behalf of Easy Garage Cleaning. Contractor shall not use the Company's name, logo, or brand for any purpose outside the scope of this Agreement without prior written consent.

  9. 9. Mutual Indemnification

    Each party agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the other party from any claims, damages, or liabilities arising out of the indemnifying party's negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of this Agreement.

  10. 10. Dispute Resolution

    Any disputes arising under this Agreement shall first be addressed through good-faith negotiation. If unresolved within thirty (30) days, disputes shall be resolved through binding mediation in Larimer County, Colorado. Colorado law governs this Agreement.

  11. 11. Electronic Signatures

    The parties agree that electronic signatures on this Agreement are valid and binding, with the same legal effect as handwritten signatures, in accordance with the federal ESIGN Act and the Colorado Uniform Electronic Transactions Act (UETA).

  12. 12. Entire Agreement

    This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements, written or oral. Any amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the date first written above.
