Privacy Policy

Easy Garage Cleaning LLC · Last updated: 2026-09-04

Information We Collect

When you submit a form, book or manage service, apply for work, or contact us, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, service address or city, project and scheduling details, message and consent records, and photos you choose to upload. Our systems may also receive basic device, browser, referring-page, advertising-attribution, and site-interaction data.

How We Use Your Information

We use this information to answer requests, conduct walkthroughs, prepare estimates, schedule and deliver service, process payments, support customers, operate employee workflows, prevent abuse, keep business records, and measure or improve our advertising and website. We do not sell or rent customer contact information. We do not use SMS opt-in data for unrelated advertising.

Cookies & Tracking

Our public marketing pages use Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, and the Meta Pixel. These providers may use cookies or similar technology and receive device identifiers, IP-derived location, page and campaign information, and interaction data for analytics and advertising measurement. Vendor libraries load after a short delay or your first interaction. You can limit cookies and interest-based advertising through your browser, device, and provider settings.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide service, maintain business and payment records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. Retention varies by record type. You may request deletion, subject to records we must or reasonably need to keep.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information you submit. Form submissions are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third Parties

We use service providers to operate the business. Depending on your interaction, information may be processed by Web3Forms for website forms; HighLevel for customer relationship and scheduling workflows; Zapier and business messaging providers for authorized automations and service communications; Firebase and Google services for private portal, operational data, and photo storage; Stripe for checkout, payments, receipts, and subscription management; and the analytics and advertising providers named above. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not receive your full payment-card number from Stripe.

SMS / Text Message Consent

Our quote and contact forms include an optional checkbox that lets you consent to receive text messages (SMS) from Easy Garage Cleaning LLC about your quote request, scheduling, and appointment updates at the phone number you provide. Consent is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message, or get assistance by replying HELP or contacting us using the information below.

No mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in data will be shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Contact us using the information below.
