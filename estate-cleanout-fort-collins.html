Situations We Handle

When families call us

Estate cleanouts aren’t one situation — they come in many forms. We’ve worked through all of them.

01

Death of a Loved One & Probate

This is the most common call we get for estate cleanouts. The property needs to be cleared — often on a timeline set by probate, a realtor, or a family agreement. We work at a pace that respects the situation. We’re used to coordinating with estate attorneys, realtors, and out-of-state family members who can’t be present the day of the job.

02

Hoarding Cleanup

Hoarding situations require patience, a non-judgmental approach, and a crew that knows what they’re doing. We’ve cleared properties that were floor-to-ceiling packed — multiple rooms, multiple decades of accumulation. We work methodically, we don’t rush, and we’re respectful of the person whose space it was.

03

Foreclosure & Bank-Owned Property

Foreclosure cleanouts often need to happen quickly to prepare a property for sale. We can work on tight timelines and coordinate directly with property managers, banks, or realtors. The property gets cleared and broom-swept so it’s ready for inspection or listing.

04

Move-Out Before Home Sale

Selling a home that’s accumulated years of belongings? Whether it’s a full cleanout or just clearing the spaces that didn’t make it onto the moving truck, we handle what’s left. Many of our estate clients are listing agents or sellers on a closing deadline — we work around your timeline.

05

Downsizing from a Large Home

Moving from a four-bedroom house into an apartment or assisted living facility means leaving behind decades of furniture, household items, and stored belongings. We handle the full haul — sorting out what goes to family, what gets donated, and what gets disposed of — so the move is one less thing to manage.

What We Do With Your Items

Donate first. Recycle second. Landfill last.

We make every effort to keep usable items out of the landfill — both because it’s the right thing to do and because it often means a meaningful tax deduction for the estate.

We try hard to give your items a second life.

When we sort through a property, we’re actively looking for items that can be donated or recycled. Usable furniture, appliances, tools, clothing, kitchenware — things that still have value go to people who need them, not to a dumpster.

We partner with Habitat for Humanity ReStore, ARC Thrift, and Goodwill for donations. After the job, we provide itemized tax donation receipts in your name. For an estate, those deductions can add up to something real.

Electronics and metals go to recycling partners. Only what genuinely can’t be salvaged ends up at the landfill — and we’re transparent about what that is.

First

Donate

Habitat for Humanity ReStore, ARC Thrift, Goodwill. Usable furniture, appliances, clothing, household goods. Itemized tax receipts in your name.

Second

Recycle

Electronics, metals, and materials that can be processed responsibly through recycling partners. Kept out of the landfill wherever possible.

Last

Dispose

What genuinely can’t be donated or recycled — hazardous material remnants, truly broken items, and waste. Handled through licensed disposal. All disposal fees are included in your flat quote.
