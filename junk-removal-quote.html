Questions

Before you book

Do you need to see it first?

Yes. We confirm the flat price when we arrive, before we lift anything. If it isn't right for you, say no and you owe nothing.

How much does it cost?

Single items run $99–150, a partial load $250–400, a standard garage cleanout $400–650, and a full garage or estate $650+. Hot tubs are $400–800 depending on size and access.

How soon can you come?

We text or call back during business hours (Mon–Sat 7am–7pm) with the soonest real opening, often this week.

Do I need to be home?

It helps, so we can walk you through the price in person. If you can't be there, text us a photo so we bring the right crew, then approve the price by text or phone when we arrive.

What happens to my stuff?

Usable items are donated and metal and recyclables are recycled. Only what's left goes to the landfill.

Which towns do you serve?

Fort Collins, Loveland, Windsor, Timnath, Wellington, Severance and LaPorte.

Do you remove hot tubs?

Yes. Power must be shut off at the breaker before we arrive (hard-wired tubs should be disconnected by a licensed electrician). We cut it up and haul it away. Decks, stairs and tight access affect the price.
