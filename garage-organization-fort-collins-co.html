FAQ

Questions we get a lot

Q.01Do you sell shelving and bins?

We recommend layouts and can install basic shelving you purchase. We don't markup products — labor-only on organization.

Q.02How much does garage organization cost in Fort Collins?

Organization add-on after cleanout: $200–$400 depending on garage size. Standalone organization (junk already gone): $250–$500.

Q.03Is organization included in a cleanout?

Basic sorting into keep/donate/haul is included. Full zone setup and labeling is an add-on service.

Q.04Do you organize two-car garages?

Yes — two-car garages are our specialty. We plan zones for both bays.

Q.05Can you organize without removing junk first?

We strongly recommend cleanout first. Organizing around junk wastes time and money.

Q.06What organization systems do you recommend?

Wall-mounted racks for bikes/tools, clear bins for seasonal, overhead storage for rarely-used items.

Q.07How long does garage organization take?

Typically 2–4 hours after the space is cleared and clean.

Q.08Do you label bins?

Yes — we label categories so everyone in the household knows where things go.

Q.09Can you organize a workshop area?

Yes — tool zones, workbench placement, and lumber storage are common requests.

Q.10Will you help maintain organization long-term?

We give you a simple maintenance checklist. Re-organize visits available on request.
