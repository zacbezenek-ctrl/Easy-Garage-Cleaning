FAQ

Questions we get a lot

Q.01 How much does junk removal cost in Fort Collins?

Most jobs fall into four ranges: single-item pickup ($99–$150), small loads ($250–$400), medium loads ($400–$650), and large hauls ($650+). Schedule a free on-site walkthrough for an exact flat-rate price.

Q.02 Do you offer next-day junk removal in Fort Collins?

Yes — when our schedule allows. we respond as quickly as we can and often book next-day in Fort Collins.

Q.03 What items can you not haul?

We cannot take liquid paint, gasoline, asbestos, medical waste, or live ammunition. See our blog guide for Fort Collins disposal options.

Q.04 Do you haul from inside the garage?

Yes. We carry items from garage, basement, or anywhere on the ground floor. Point us to what's going.

Q.05 Is there a minimum load size?

No minimum for walkthrough-based pricing. Single-item pickups start around $99–$150 depending on item and access.

Q.06 Do you donate usable junk?

Yes — furniture and household goods in good shape go to Habitat ReStore, ARC, and Goodwill. You get the tax receipt.

Q.07 How do I schedule a walkthrough?

schedule a free on-site walkthrough online or by text. Wide shots of the full pile work best.

Q.08 Are you insured in Fort Collins?

Yes. Easy Garage Cleaning LLC is a Colorado-registered LLC with general liability and commercial auto insurance.

Q.09 Do you serve Loveland and Windsor too?

Yes — Fort Collins is home base, but we serve Loveland, Windsor, Timnath, and Wellington daily.

Q.10 What happens to my junk after pickup?

Donations first, then Larimer County Landfill or recycling for the rest. We never dump illegally.
