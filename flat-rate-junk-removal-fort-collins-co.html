FAQ

Curbside pickup questions

Q.01 How much does curbside junk pickup cost in Fort Collins?

$139 flat for up to 4 cubic yards, as long as items are at the curb or driveway. No on-site estimate and no hidden fees.

Q.02 How much is 4 cubic yards of junk?

About a fully loaded full-size pickup truck, or roughly one-quarter of a standard junk-removal truck — enough for a small garage corner or single-room cleanout.

Q.03 Do I need to be home for curbside pickup?

No. Set your items at the curb or driveway, and our crew loads and hauls them — you don't have to be present.

Q.04 What's the difference between curbside and full-service junk removal?

Curbside means you place items outside; we skip the in-home labor and pass the savings on, which is why it's a flat $139. Full-service means we carry items out of the garage, basement, or house for a flat-rate photo quote.

Q.05 Is flat-rate junk removal cheaper than pay-by-volume?

Usually, yes. Volume pricing requires an on-site estimate and can climb; our $139 covers up to 4 cubic yards with no surprises.

Q.06 What can I put out for the $139 curbside pickup?

Furniture, mattresses, appliances, boxes, yard debris, and general household junk. Hazardous materials, liquid paint, and chemicals aren't accepted.

Q.07 What if my junk is more than 4 cubic yards?

We'll quote a flat rate for the larger load — just text photos when you book and we'll size it. Most garage cleanouts run $400–$3,500 flat-rate.

Q.08 How soon can you pick up?

We respond to every request within 5 minutes and book the earliest available curbside slot — often next-day, depending on schedule.
