  1. 1. Scope of Services

    Contractor shall serve as an independent lead setter for the Company. Contractor's duties include but are not limited to:

    • Responding to inbound customer inquiries (calls, texts, form submissions, and messages) on behalf of the Company.
    • Qualifying prospective customers and gathering necessary job details (address, scope, timeline).
    • Scheduling and booking appointments for garage cleanouts, junk removal, and related services.
    • Following Company-provided scripts, pricing guidelines, and customer communication standards.
    • Maintaining accurate records of all leads contacted, response times, and appointments booked.

  2. 2. Compensation

    Company shall pay Contractor according to the following commission structure:

    • $30.00 per booked appointment — A "booked appointment" is defined as a confirmed, scheduled service appointment with a qualified customer that appears on the Company's calendar.
    • $3.00 per rapid response — Paid for each unique customer inquiry that Contractor responds to within two (2) minutes of the inquiry being received, regardless of whether the inquiry converts to a booked appointment.

    Payment shall be issued on a bi-weekly basis via _______________ (Venmo / Zelle / check / direct deposit). Company will provide Contractor with a summary of qualifying leads and responses for each pay period. Contractor has five (5) business days after receiving the summary to dispute any discrepancies.

    As an independent contractor, Contractor is responsible for all federal, state, and local taxes on earnings. Company will issue a 1099-NEC form at year-end if total payments equal or exceed $600.

  3. 3. Independent Contractor Status

    Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner, or agent of the Company. Contractor shall:

    • Control the manner and means by which services are performed.
    • Set their own schedule, provided response-time targets are met.
    • Provide their own phone, computer, and internet access.
    • Not be entitled to employee benefits, workers' compensation, unemployment insurance, or any other employee benefits from the Company.

  4. 4. Term and Termination

    This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues on an at-will basis. Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, by providing seven (7) days written notice (text or email is acceptable). Upon termination:

    • Company shall pay Contractor for all qualifying leads and responses completed through the termination date.
    • Contractor shall return or delete any Company materials, customer lists, login credentials, and proprietary information within 48 hours.

  5. 5. Confidentiality

    Contractor agrees to keep confidential all non-public business information, including but not limited to: customer lists, contact information, pricing structures, marketing strategies, scripts, and internal processes. This obligation survives termination of this Agreement for a period of two (2) years.

  6. 6. Non-Solicitation

    For a period of twelve (12) months following termination of this Agreement, Contractor shall not directly or indirectly:

    • Solicit, contact, or attempt to divert any customer or prospective customer of the Company for the purpose of providing competing services (garage cleanouts, junk removal, hauling, or related services).
    • Encourage or induce any other contractor or employee of the Company to leave or reduce their engagement with the Company.

  7. 7. Performance Standards

    To qualify for the rapid-response bonus, Contractor must respond within two (2) minutes of the inquiry timestamp as recorded by the Company's CRM, phone system, or messaging platform. Company's system records shall be the sole authority for determining response times.

  8. 8. Use of Company Brand

    When communicating with customers, Contractor shall represent themselves as acting on behalf of Easy Garage Cleaning. Contractor shall not use the Company's name, logo, or brand for any purpose outside the scope of this Agreement without prior written consent.

  9. 9. Indemnification

    Contractor agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the Company from any claims, damages, or liabilities arising out of Contractor's negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of this Agreement.

  10. 10. Dispute Resolution

    Any disputes arising under this Agreement shall first be addressed through good-faith negotiation. If unresolved within thirty (30) days, disputes shall be resolved through binding mediation in Larimer County, Colorado. Colorado law governs this Agreement.

  11. 11. Entire Agreement

    This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements, written or oral. Any amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.
