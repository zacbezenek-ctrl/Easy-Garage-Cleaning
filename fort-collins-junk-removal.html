What We Don't Haul

A Short List of No-Goes

Most things, we take. But there are a handful of materials that require licensed hazmat disposal — and we won't pretend otherwise.

We cannot accept these items:

  • Asbestos — requires certified hazmat abatement professionals. Found in older insulation, floor tiles, and ceiling materials in pre-1980 homes.
  • Live or wet paint — dried latex paint in small quantities is usually fine, but gallons of wet or oil-based paint require hazardous waste drop-off. Larimer County has periodic collection events.
  • Medical waste — sharps, syringes, biohazardous materials. These must go through licensed medical waste disposal channels.
  • Ammunition & explosives — contact your local law enforcement or use a licensed gun shop for safe disposal of live rounds and related materials.
  • Motor oil in large quantities — small amounts in sealed containers may be fine; large drums or open containers of used motor oil need to go to a recycling center (AutoZone and O'Reilly accept it free).

Not sure if your item qualifies? Call us at (970) 999-1818 and we'll tell you honestly — no runaround, no showing up and charging you before refusing the load.
