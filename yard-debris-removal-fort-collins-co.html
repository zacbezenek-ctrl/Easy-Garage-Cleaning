FAQ

Questions we get a lot

Q.01 How much does yard debris removal cost in Fort Collins?

Small piles and single items: $99–$250. Large branch piles or multiple items: $250–$400. Full garage + yard combo: $400–$650+.

Q.02 Do you take tree branches?

Yes — branches, brush, and storm debris. Very large logs may need a custom quote.

Q.03 Can you remove yard debris from my garage?

Yes — lawn equipment and outdoor furniture stored in garages is extremely common.

Q.04 Do you take gas-powered lawn equipment?

Yes — mowers, trimmers, blowers. Please drain fuel if possible; we can't haul full gas cans.

Q.05 Is yard debris different from junk removal?

Same crew, same flat-rate approach. Yard debris is just our label for outdoor/garage-adjacent items.

Q.06 Do you take dirt or rocks?

Small amounts yes. Large soil/rock loads need a custom quote due to weight.

Q.07 Can you haul fencing materials?

Yes — old fence sections, posts, and lumber from Fort Collins properties.

Q.08 Next-day yard debris pickup?

Often available for smaller loads. Text photos of the pile.

Q.09 Do you compost or recycle yard waste?

Green waste goes to appropriate Larimer County facilities — not mixed with landfill trash.

Q.10 Can yard debris be part of a garage cleanout?

Yes — outdoor items in the garage are included in full cleanout quotes.
