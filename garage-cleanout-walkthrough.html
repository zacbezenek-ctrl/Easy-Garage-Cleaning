Questions

Garage cleanout FAQ

Do I need to sort before you arrive?

No. Mark what stays and point to what is going. We handle sorting, carrying, and loading.

What if I want to keep some items?

We do a walkthrough first. You tell us what is staying, and we only haul what you approve.

Is the price flat-rate?

Yes. No hourly billing. We set one flat price during the free walkthrough, and labor, hauling, dump fees, and donation drop-offs are included.

Do you sweep the garage floor after?

Yes. A basic sweep is included so you walk into a clear, usable space.

Can you clean out attached and detached garages?

Yes, both. Detached garages and workshops are common in Old Town and Midtown Fort Collins.

Do you donate usable items?

Yes. Usable items go to Habitat ReStore, ARC, and Goodwill, and you receive a donation receipt in your name.
