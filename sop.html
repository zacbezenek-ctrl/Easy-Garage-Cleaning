Section 1

How a Lead Enters the System

Leads come in from two sources:

📘 Facebook Ads
Customer fills out a Facebook Lead Ad form. Zapier instantly picks it up, texts Tyler + Zac, creates a contact in Quo, and saves the lead to Firestore.
📗 Google Sheet / Manual
A lead is manually added to the EGC Google Sheet (used for walk-ins, referrals, or imports). Same result — texts go out, contact created, Firestore updated.

Once a lead is in Firestore, the automation begins.

What is Firestore? Firestore is EGC's lead database. Every customer has a record there. All Zaps read and write to it. Tyler doesn't need to touch Firestore directly — the system manages it.
Section 2

The Automation Sequence

Here's what happens to a lead automatically if Tyler doesn't engage:

  1. Lead arrives — texted to Tyler + Zac instantly via SMS. A contact is created in Quo.
  2. Hour 1+ — No-Answer Cadence fires every hour. If the lead hasn't responded and Tyler hasn't engaged, an automated SMS goes out (Attempt 0).
  3. Subsequent hours — Up to 4 more automated follow-up texts (Attempts 1–4) go out at set intervals if there's still no reply.
  4. Lead replies — Tyler is notified. Automation pauses. Tyler takes over.
  5. Quote sent — Quote Follow-Up cadence starts: 4 rounds of automated follow-up texts over several days if the customer doesn't book.
  6. Customer books — Booking confirmation SMS sent immediately, reminder SMS sent before the job.
  7. Job completed — Tyler marks it done in the employee portal. 24 hours later, the customer gets a Google review request SMS.
Key Point Steps 2–4 (the No-Answer Cadence) only fire when conversationActive = false. The moment Tyler engages, the system steps back. See Section 6.
Section 3

Tyler's Role — Working Leads Manually

When Tyler Should Jump In

  • When a lead replies to an automated text
  • When a lead calls in directly
  • When Zac assigns a lead to follow up on
  • When a lead is hot and Tyler wants to skip the automated cadence

How Tyler Works a Lead

  1. Tyler sees the lead notification SMS with the customer's number.
  2. Tyler opens Quo and finds the conversation.
  3. Tyler texts or calls the customer from Quo using the EGC number (970) 999-1818.
  4. The moment Tyler sends a text via Quo, the system automatically sets conversationActive = true — automation pauses for that lead.
  5. Tyler works the lead — qualifies, schedules a site visit, gives a quote.
  6. When done (call ended, conversation over), Tyler texts a command to update the system. See Section 4.
Important Always work leads from Quo using the EGC number. Do not text customers from your personal number — the system can't track it and the lead will remain in the automated sequence.
Section 4

Tyler's Text Commands

Tyler controls the system by texting commands to the EGC Quo number (970) 999-1818 from his personal phone (925) 457-4584. These only work from Tyler's number — the system ignores the same commands from anyone else.

Format Text: COMMAND {10-digit phone number}
Example: CALL 9704441234
You'll get a confirmation text back confirming the update.
Command When to Use It What It Does
CALL 9701234567 The moment you get on a call with a lead Sets conversationActive = true. Pauses all automated follow-ups for that lead. Logs call start time.
DONE 9701234567 After a call ends with no definitive outcome Sets conversationActive = false. Resumes automation. Logs last contact time.
BOOKED 9701234567 Customer confirmed the job Sets status to booked. Stops all follow-up sequences permanently. Logs the booking.
LOST 9701234567 Customer said no, ghosted, or went with someone else Sets status to lost. Stops all follow-up sequences permanently.
Note on calls When a call ends via Quo, the system automatically clears conversationActive — so DONE is only needed if you used your personal phone or if you want to manually close a conversation without a call.

Section 5

The Employee Portal

The employee portal lives at easygaragecleaning.com/employee. Log in with your credentials. It shows:

  • CRM Dashboard — weekly calendar view of all scheduled jobs
  • Jobs list — all active leads with status, customer name, phone, address
  • Admin section — Zac-only controls

Marking a Job Complete

This is the most important thing Tyler does in the portal. When the job is done:

  1. Find the job in the portal (calendar or jobs list).
  2. Click "Mark Complete" on the job card.
  3. The system automatically fires the review request sequence — 24 hours later, the customer gets a Google review request SMS. No action needed from Tyler.
Don't skip this step. If you don't mark the job complete, the customer never gets a review request. Reviews are critical for EGC's Google ranking. Every completed job should be marked immediately on-site or right after.
Section 6

The conversationActive Flag — The Guard System

Every lead in Firestore has a field called conversationActive — it's either true or false. This is what prevents the automation from texting customers while Tyler is actively working them.

What Happens conversationActive Changes To
Tyler sends a text to a lead via Quo true (automatic)
Tyler texts CALL {phone} true (manual)
A call ends on the EGC Quo number false (automatic)
Tyler texts DONE {phone} false (manual)
Tyler texts BOOKED {phone} false + status = booked
Tyler texts LOST {phone} false + status = lost

When conversationActive = true, the No-Answer Cadence and Quote Follow-Up Zaps skip that lead entirely. The system will not text the customer while Tyler is in an active conversation.

Section 7

After the Job — Reviews & Follow-Up

Review Requests (Automatic)

The moment Tyler clicks "Mark Complete" in the employee portal:

  1. The system checks if the customer has opted out of texts. If yes — nothing happens.
  2. It waits exactly 24 hours.
  3. It re-checks opt-out status (in case they opted out during the wait).
  4. It sends this SMS from the EGC number:
"Hi [FirstName], thanks for using EGC garage cleaning! Would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? It really helps our small business: [Google Review Link] - Tyler & the EGC team"

Tyler does not need to do anything else. The review request is fully automated.

Opt-Outs

If a customer texts any of the following words, they are immediately removed from all automated sequences and Tyler is notified:

STOP UNSUBSCRIBE CANCEL QUIT END

Any capitalization works — "stop", "Stop", "STOP" all work. Tyler gets a text: "[Customer] opted out. DO NOT CONTACT." Do not message that customer again.

Section 8

What Tyler Should Never Do

❌ Text customers from your personal phone
The system can't see personal texts. The lead stays in the automated sequence and will get both your personal texts and automated follow-ups — double contact.
❌ Ignore the BOOKED and LOST commands
If you book a job without texting BOOKED, the Quote Follow-Up sequence keeps running and will text the customer "Have you had a chance to think about our quote?" even after they've booked. That looks bad.
❌ Skip marking jobs complete in the portal
No Mark Complete = no review request. Reviews directly impact how many leads EGC gets from Google. Every job needs to be marked.
❌ Contact customers who have opted out
If a customer texted STOP, do not reach out via any channel. The system blocks automated texts but Tyler is responsible for not contacting them manually either.
