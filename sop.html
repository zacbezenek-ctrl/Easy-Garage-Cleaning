Section 1

How a Lead Enters the System

  1. Customer fills out an EGC Facebook ad form (or is added to the system manually).
  2. The system instantly texts the customer a greeting from Tyler — Tyler doesn't have to do anything, it just fires.
  3. Tyler gets an SMS alert immediately with the customer's name, number, and what they need.
  4. The lead is saved to Firestore (EGC's database) and appears in the Employee Portal under the Leads tab.
  5. The clock starts. Call the lead as fast as possible — speed wins, and the customer has already been promised a call.
Instant Greeting SMS (NEW) Within seconds of the lead hitting the system, the customer receives one of two texts from the EGC number:

Between 8 AM–9 PM MT: "Hey! This is Tyler from Easy Garage Cleaning — I'll give you a call in just a couple minutes! 🙌"

Outside calling hours: "Hey! This is Tyler from Easy Garage Cleaning — we're outside calling hours right now, but I'll give you a call first thing tomorrow morning! 🙌"
Follow through on the promise The customer was just told Tyler is calling in "a couple minutes" (or "first thing tomorrow morning"). Honor that. If you can't call within 2 minutes during business hours, you've already broken the first impression. First thing tomorrow = before 9 AM.

ButtonWhen to press itWhat it does
✅ BOOKED Customer confirmed the job — date set, deposit taken (if applicable) Sets status to Booked. Stops all follow-up automation permanently for this lead.
❌ LOST Customer said no, went with someone else, or stopped responding after multiple attempts Sets status to Lost. Stops all automation. Asks for a quick loss reason (optional).
⏸ PAUSE AUTO Tyler is actively in conversation with this lead and doesn't want automated messages firing Sets conversationActive = true. Pauses automation for this lead. Changes to ▶ RESUME AUTO when active.
Key rule Every lead should end up as BOOKED or LOST. Leads that just sit there with no update are wasted opportunities and create noise in the system.
Section 4

Meta Conversion Signals — Click One on Every Lead

Below the BOOKED / LOST / PAUSE buttons in the lead drawer are four signal buttons that match Facebook's standard lead-stage values. These feed data back to Meta so the ad algorithm learns what good leads look like and finds more of them. Tyler should click one of these on EVERY lead — it's how the ads get smarter and cheaper over time.

👍 QUALIFIEDReal lead, worth pursuing
💰 CONVERTEDBooked & paid
🚫 NOT INTERESTEDReal person, said no
🗂 ARCHIVEDJunk / spam / unreachable
ButtonWhen to click it
👍 QUALIFIED The lead is real and worth pursuing — right area, reasonable budget, real job — even if they haven't booked yet.
💰 CONVERTED Lead actually booked and paid (or scheduled a paid job). This is the strongest signal — Meta uses it to find more leads like this one.
🚫 NOT INTERESTED You reached the customer, the lead was real, but they said no, went with someone else, or aren't ready right now.
🗂 ARCHIVED Junk lead — spam, fake info, wrong service area, totally unreachable after multiple attempts, or any reason this lead can't progress.
Why this matters Every signal you send teaches Facebook's algorithm. Skip this and the ads stay dumb — they keep finding tire-kickers because they have no idea what a good EGC lead looks like. Click one on every lead and watch cost-per-lead drop over the following weeks.
NOT INTERESTED vs ARCHIVED NOT INTERESTED = you got through, they're a real person, they declined. ARCHIVED = the lead was junk from the start (wrong number, fake info, totally unreachable). The difference matters — Meta treats "real-person-said-no" very differently from "this was never a real lead."
Section 5

Pausing and Resuming Automation

When Tyler starts working a lead, he can pause the automation so the system doesn't fire any messages while he's in an active conversation.

When to pause

  • You've called the lead and are waiting for them to call back
  • You've sent a text and are in an active back-and-forth
  • Any time you don't want the system sending automated messages to this lead

How to pause

  1. Open the Employee Portal → Leads tab
  2. Find the lead and tap/click to open their detail drawer
  3. Tap ⏸ PAUSE AUTO — it turns into ▶ RESUME AUTO

When automation resumes automatically

When a call ends on the EGC Quo number, the system automatically resumes automation for that lead. Tyler can also tap ▶ RESUME AUTO manually at any time.

Note When Tyler sends a text to a lead via Quo, the system automatically pauses automation for that lead. So just texting from Quo is usually enough — no need to manually hit Pause unless Tyler is working the lead in a way the system can't detect (like calling from his personal phone).
Section 6

Scheduling & Recurring Appointments

Jobs are scheduled in the Schedule tab of the Employee Portal. When creating a new job, there's a Repeat dropdown with these options:

  • One-time — default; single job, no repeat
  • Weekly — every 7 days
  • Every 2 Weeks — biweekly
  • Monthly — auto-generates 6 future instances on the calendar
  • Quarterly — every 3 months

Recurring jobs show a 🔁 icon on the calendar so they're easy to spot.

Use this for ongoing accounts Monthly garage cleanouts for property managers, biweekly storage facility sweeps, quarterly HOA jobs — any account that repeats on a schedule should be set up as a recurring appointment. Set it once and the calendar fills itself.
Section 7

Marking Jobs Complete & Review Requests

This is one of the most important things Tyler does. Every completed job needs to be marked in the portal.

  1. Job is done. Tyler opens the Employee Portal.
  2. Go to the Jobs tab or the calendar view.
  3. Find the job and click "Mark Complete."
  4. Done. The system handles everything else.

What happens automatically after Mark Complete:

  • The job status updates to Completed in Firestore.
  • The system waits exactly 24 hours.
  • The customer receives this SMS from the EGC number:
"Hi [FirstName], thanks for using EGC garage cleaning! Would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? It really helps our small business: [Google Review Link] — Tyler & the EGC team"
Don't skip this. Reviews are EGC's #1 source of organic leads. Every job that isn't marked complete = a review request that never goes out. It takes 5 seconds and it compounds over time.
Section 8

Opt-Outs — What to Do

If a customer texts any of these words, they are immediately removed from all automated messages and Tyler gets an alert:

STOP stop UNSUBSCRIBE CANCEL QUIT END

Any capitalization works. The system is case-insensitive.

Tyler receives: "[Customer] opted out. DO NOT CONTACT."

❌ Do not contact opted-out customers
Once someone opts out, do not text or call them via EGC channels. The system blocks automated messages automatically. Tyler is responsible for not contacting them manually either. This is a legal requirement (TCPA).
Section 9

The 4 Golden Rules

1. Always work leads from Quo — never your personal phone.
The system can't track personal phone calls or texts. The lead stays in an unknown state and things break. Every customer interaction should come from the EGC number (970) 999-1818 via Quo.
2. Always mark leads BOOKED or LOST when resolved.
Open the portal, find the lead, hit the button. Takes 10 seconds. If you don't do this, the lead just sits there forever — it's noise that makes the system harder to manage and wastes everyone's time.
3. Always click Mark Complete after every job.
Every job that gets marked complete generates a review request automatically. Every review helps EGC rank higher on Google. Higher ranking = more leads = more jobs for Tyler. It compounds.
4. Always click a Meta signal on every lead (QUALIFIED / CONVERTED / NOT INTERESTED / ARCHIVED).
Every signal teaches the Facebook ad algorithm. Skip it and the ads stay dumb — they keep finding tire-kickers. Pick the right bucket: Qualified = real and pursuing, Converted = booked & paid, Not Interested = real person said no, Archived = junk lead.
