How treadmill & exercise equipment removal works

That treadmill that turned into a clothes rack, the elliptical nobody touches, the weight bench buried under boxes — exercise equipment is some of the heaviest, most awkward stuff in any Fort Collins garage. A loaded squat rack or a commercial-grade treadmill can top 250 pounds, with motors, decks, and steel uprights that make a one-person move a real injury risk. Our crew handles the entire job: we assess the piece, disassemble what needs to come apart to clear doorways and stairs, carry it out, load it, and clean up after. You never have to touch it. For a deeper look at how flat-rate hauling works across the board, see our main junk removal in Fort Collins page.

Every job follows the same three steps: text photos for a 5-minute response, approve your locked flat-rate quote, and we show up and haul it. There is no truck-visit estimate required for most single items and no hourly meter running while we work. If you want a fixed price you can see before you ever call, our $139 flat-rate curbside pickup covers a single item already disconnected and waiting at the curb or just inside the garage door.

Equipment we remove and what affects the cost

We take treadmills (folding and standard), ellipticals, stair climbers, Peloton and spin bikes, rowing machines, weight benches, squat racks, free weights, cable machines, and full multi-station home gyms. A single bulky item is flat $99–$150. A whole home gym with several machines is quoted as a small load, typically $250–$400. Three things move a quote within those ranges: weight and disassembly (a 300-pound smith machine takes more to break down than a folding treadmill), access (a basement carry-out up a tight staircase is more labor than a garage roll-out), and total volume if you are clearing several pieces at once. Weight is always built into the flat rate up front — there are never per-item or heavy-item surcharges added on the day. See the full breakdown on our pricing page, or browse everything we take.

Often a treadmill is just the start. Once the equipment is gone, a lot of customers have us clear the rest of the bay at the same time — old shelving, broken furniture, boxes. If that sounds like your garage, our furniture removal in Fort Collins service pairs naturally with an equipment haul, and usable pieces are routed to local charities or a licensed recycler instead of the landfill whenever possible.

FAQ

Q.01 How much does treadmill removal cost in Fort Collins?

A single treadmill, elliptical, or weight bench typically runs $99–$150 flat-rate. A full home gym with several machines is quoted as a small load, usually $250–$400 depending on volume and weight.

Q.02 Do you take ellipticals, Pelotons, weight benches, and full home gyms?

Yes — treadmills, ellipticals, stair climbers, Peloton and spin bikes, rowing machines, weight benches, squat racks, free weights, and full multi-station home gym systems. If it is exercise equipment, we haul it.

Q.03 Do you disassemble treadmills and gym equipment?

Yes. Our crew does basic disassembly — folding decks, removing uprights, breaking down racks — when it is needed to clear doorways and stairs safely. You do not have to take anything apart yourself.

Q.04 Can you carry equipment up from a basement or down from an upstairs room?

Yes. Basement and upstairs carry-outs are common for home gyms. Send photos of the equipment and the stairs or doorway so we can confirm access and lock your flat-rate quote before we arrive.

Q.05 What does it cost for one item versus a full home gym?

One bulky item is flat $99–$150. A full home gym or several machines falls into our small-load range of $250–$400. Every quote is flat-rate from photos with no hourly billing and no per-item surcharges.

Q.06 Do you donate working exercise equipment?

When equipment is in usable condition we route it to local charities or a licensed recycler instead of the landfill whenever possible. Ask when you request your quote and we will let you know the options.

Q.07 How fast can you pick up a treadmill?

Q.08 Is there a surcharge for heavy equipment?

No. Weight is already factored into your flat-rate quote upfront — there are no per-item or heavy-item surcharges added on the day of the job.

Q.09 Do I need to prep or move the equipment first?

No prep needed. Leave it where it sits — our insured crew does all the lifting and protects floors, walls, and doorways during carry-out.
