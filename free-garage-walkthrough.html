What happens on the walkthrough

A useful plan first. The decision is yours.

We diagnose the whole garage, design the result, and then let you decide whether you want our crew to execute it.

  1. We confirm a time

    After you send the form, we call or text to set a walkthrough time that works for you.

  2. We walk the space together

    You show us what stays, what goes, and what you want the garage to do better. Plan on about 15 to 20 minutes.

  3. You get your Garage Turnaround Plan

    We map removal, cleaning, storage, organization, and prevention into one clear plan with one bundled price.

  4. You choose your path

    Keep the plan and do it yourself, or approve the price and let our crew complete the turnaround from start to finish.

Questions

Before you book

Is the walkthrough really free?

Yes. The Garage Turnaround Plan is completely free, and the plan is yours whether or not you hire us.

How much does a Garage Turnaround cost?

Cleanouts and turnarounds start at $675. Every garage is different, so we set one bundled flat price after the walkthrough. You approve it before any work begins, with no hourly meter.

Do I need to be home?

You need to be present for the walkthrough, which takes about 15 to 20 minutes.

What do you haul away?

Furniture, appliances, mattresses, boxes, tools, yard debris, and general household junk. We cannot haul hazardous materials like liquid paint, asbestos, or medical waste.

Do you donate usable items?

Where practical, we route usable items for donation or recycling. Acceptance depends on the receiving organization and the condition of the items.

Are you insured?

Ask our team for current insurance details before your project.
