FAQ

Questions we get a lot

Q.01 How much does a house cleanout cost in Fort Collins?

It depends on volume — most whole-house cleanouts fall between $650 and $3,500. Text photos or a room count for an exact flat-rate quote.

Q.02 Do you handle estate and downsizing cleanouts?

Yes, with care and discretion. We work with families, executors, and realtors to clear the property on your timeline.

Q.03 Can you clear a property for a move-out or closing deadline?

Yes — we respond in 5 minutes and schedule around your closing or lease date, often same or next day.

Q.04 Do you do foreclosure and rental cleanouts?

Yes. We handle bank-owned, landlord, and abandoned-property cleanouts with flat-rate quotes and clean invoicing.

Q.05 What do you do with everything?

Usable items go to ReStore, ARC, and Goodwill with a donation receipt in your name. The rest is disposed of or recycled responsibly.

Q.06 Do I need to sort or be there?

No sorting needed. Mark anything staying and we handle the rest. We can coordinate access if you can't be on site.

Q.07 Is it flat-rate?

Yes. The price we quote from your photos is the price you pay — no hourly billing.
