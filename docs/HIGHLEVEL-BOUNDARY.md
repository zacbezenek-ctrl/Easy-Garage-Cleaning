## Owner rule

> "don't fuck with GHL, all follow-ups and customer communication goes through high level, we just need tracking through high level" (owner, 2026-09-28)

HighLevel (GHL) owns every follow-up and every customer message. The Employee Hub (Cloudflare Pages Functions) and the Railway platform (egc-api, egc-mcp, egc-worker) track what happens. They do not run a second follow-up queue, and they never write to HighLevel as a side effect of something that is not a real send the owner turned on.

This page lists every code path that writes to HighLevel or messages a customer, with its flag and default. Every row was checked again against the code after the integration branch (FUN-13, SYNC-QUEUE, CLIENT-LOGIN, FUN-36, RECUR-CRON, FUN-30 and the rest) was merged into GHL-ALIGN on 2026-09-29. Line references are given so each row can be checked again. The same paths are registered, with their HighLevel triggers, in [automation-registry.md](automation-registry.md).

Two terms are used below. **Hub operations** is the Hub side of the signed bridge: it is on whenever `HUB_SESSION_SECRET` is 32+ characters, unless Cloudflare `EGC_OPERATIONS_ENABLED` is set to anything other than `true`. **The bridge is on** means Hub operations are on and egc-api has `EGC_OPERATIONS_ENABLED=true` (off by default; [go-live B5](GO-LIVE.md#b5-railway-platform-and-the-signed-bridge) turns it on).

## The HighLevel tag outbox (GHL-TRACK-1)

HighLevel's confirmations and reminders start from the tags the Hub adds. Until now only the browser added them (row 2, `tool=schedule`), from a manager's open Hub, three visits per page load. With the server schedule mirror on (row 17) the browser skips the mirrored visits, so no tag was added and confirmations and reminders silently stopped. Cancel and No-show changed nothing in HighLevel, and walkthrough outcomes were never told.

With `EGC_GHL_TAG_OUTBOX=true` on Cloudflare Pages (off by default), the Hub keeps a durable outbox. It still only adds tags and sets the appointment status. It sends no message, and it never moves a pipeline stage or creates an opportunity.

- **Written with the change.** A dispatch save (`functions/_lib/dispatch-service.js`, which also covers the signed walkthrough handoff, quote drafts and recurring-plan visits), a bridge schedule change (`functions/_lib/operations-scheduling.js`) and a walkthrough outcome (`functions/_lib/walkthrough-visit.js`) write one `ghlTagOutbox` entry in the same Firestore commit as the change, or not at all. The entry id is derived from the visit and its change key (the request id), and it is created once. The visit points at its newest entry (`ghlTagEntry`).
- **Told once.** The API that saved the change makes the first attempt after its response (`ctx.waitUntil`). The Railway egc-worker (`egc-platform/apps/worker/src/ghl-tag-worker.ts`, `EGC_GHL_TAG_DRAIN_ENABLED`, off) calls `POST /api/ghl-tag-drain` every 2 minutes with the messaging cron's signed service auth (a v2 envelope bound to that path, a single-use nonce, the `ghl-tag-worker` actor). Each attempt claims the entry first, so two drains never both write. An attempt re-reads the visit: a change the visit has since moved past (moved again, cancelled, restored) is closed without any HighLevel request.
- **The appointment comes first, as today.** Today the browser adds the booking and reminder tags right after it writes the HighLevel appointment, in the same request. A booking, move or restore therefore waits while the visit's appointment write is still to come (its `syncStatus` is `pending`, `syncing` or `error`) and is told once the calendar mirror has written it: the page's sync (row 2) hands the entry its attempt at once, and after the schedule-sync worker (row 17) the entry is told at its next check (1, 2, 5, 10, 15, 30, then every 60 minutes). Waiting is not a failure: it never parks, and it ends, untold, when the visit starts, as with the flag off when an appointment is never written. A booking, move or restore whose visit has no HighLevel appointment and none coming (a `not_needed` visit whose customer was linked later, or a mirror that finished without one) is closed untold (`not_mirrored`): with the flag off these tags go only with an appointment. A walkthrough outcome does not wait.
- **Never a cancel for a booking HighLevel never heard of.** A cancel or no-show is told only when HighLevel has the visit's appointment or was told an earlier time of it (the same chain of earlier entries that decides `egc-visit-rescheduled`). Otherwise, for example a visit cancelled minutes after it was booked, before its appointment was written, the entry is closed untold (`never_told`) with no request, so a customer who never got a confirmation is never told the visit was cancelled. While the visit's earlier change is being told at that very moment (its claim is live), the cancel or the move waits for it (`earlier_change`, not an attempt).
- **Backoff and parking.** Failed attempts wait 1, 5, 15 and then 60 minutes. After 8 attempts the entry is parked. The Command center then shows **HighLevel tags stuck for N visits** with **Retry**, and the dispatch card shows **HighLevel stuck** with **Retry**. A pending entry the drain has not attempted 15 minutes after it was due counts as stuck too (the tag worker has stopped or fallen far behind), so a stopped worker never looks like the normal first minutes of **HighLevel waiting**. Only changes that are still what the visit shows are counted: a parked or overdue change the visit has since moved past (cancelled and told, moved, started) is closed without a request when the Command center reads the count, and the card shows nothing for it. Retry gives parked and overdue entries a fresh 8 attempts and tells HighLevel at once, up to 5 visits per press. A storage failure during an attempt is recorded as `ghl_tag_storage_unavailable`, a HighLevel refusal as `highlevel_<status>`.
- **No contact is created in a messaging dry run.** An entry for a visit with no linked HighLevel contact (neither on the visit nor on its customer) is an ordinary failure, `ghl_tag_contact_not_linked`: it is retried on the same backoff and parks after 8 attempts. Every attempt reads the links again, so a contact linked meanwhile (the browser sync, customer-resolve, adoption) is used without a Retry. Only with `EGC_MESSAGING_DRY_RUN=false` does the outbox upsert the contact, as the browser sync does. A visit the Hub never mirrors to HighLevel (Dispatch's `syncStatus: 'not_needed'`: a customer with no contact and no appointment) has nothing to tell: its entry is closed at once, as with the flag off, and never counts as stuck.
- **The browser and the outbox never both add the tags.** When a visit's newest entry was written at its current time, the browser booking sync (`tool=schedule`, and a Game Plan's job date) reads that entry. Already told: the sync mirrors the appointment and adds no tag (`automation.tagOwner: 'outbox'`, `tagSynced: true`). Still to tell, waiting or parked: the sync mirrors the appointment, links the contact, and then hands the entry one attempt (a parked entry gets a fresh 8 attempts), so the tags follow the appointment in the same request's background work (`tagOwner: 'outbox'`, `tagStatus: 'queued'`, `tagSynced: false`; `tagSynced` is true only for tags already added). Closed without telling HighLevel, or missing: the browser adds the tags itself, as with the flag off. The outbox tags only the contact linked to the visit or its customer; a contact the sync could not verify against the visit's phone or email is not linked, so its entry parks for a person instead of tagging an unverified contact.
- **A stopped tag worker shows.** Each signed drain tick that completes a pass records the worker's check-in (`ghlTagDrainState/worker`, server-only). With none in the last 10 minutes the Command center says **The HighLevel tag worker has not run for N minutes** (or **has not run yet**), with or without stuck visits. It goes stale when egc-worker is down, `EGC_GHL_TAG_DRAIN_ENABLED` is not `true`, the Hub is not in v2 service auth, `API_BEARER_TOKEN` differs between Railway and the Hub, or HighLevel is not configured on Pages. Until it runs again, only the save's first attempt and a manager's Retry tell HighLevel.
- **Closed visits send no calendar notice.** With the flag on, the booking sync writes a cancelled or no-show visit's appointment as cancelled or noshow with `toNotify: false`, as the outbox does, instead of re-confirming it, and never creates an appointment for one.
- **Dispatch shows it.** Each card shows **HighLevel told 2:14 PM**, **HighLevel waiting** or **HighLevel stuck · Retry** (parked, or overdue while the tag worker is stopped). Retry is offered only to a viewer who can manage dispatch (owner or manager, `ghlTagRetry` on the Hub's dispatch read); anyone else who can see the board sees **HighLevel stuck** without it. The Cancel and No-show dialogs say "HighLevel will be told (appointment marked cancelled/no-show). Your HighLevel workflow decides what the customer hears."

**Cleanouts and reorganizations.** The browser sync sends the visit's own type as `event_type`, and only `job` counts as a job there, so a cleanout or reorg visit is tagged `egc-walkthrough-scheduled` (and its appointment goes on the walkthrough calendar). The outbox keeps that mapping on purpose, so turning the outbox on does not change which of the owner's workflows (and which customer message) a cleanout or reorg booking starts. Tagging them `egc-job-scheduled` is probably right, but it changes the customer's message and belongs in its own flagged change.

A cancelled or no-show visit's tag is added whatever **Notify customer** says, like today's scheduled tags; HighLevel's workflow decides whether the customer hears anything. HighLevel starts a tag workflow only when the tag is new on the contact, as it does today for the browser's tags. The new tags (`egc-visit-*`, `egc-walkthrough-no-show`, `egc-walkthrough-lost`, `egc-quote-to-follow`) start nothing until the owner builds a workflow on them. The Scheduled stage move still comes only from the browser sync (row 2); the outbox moves no stage. Completion and money milestones are GHL-TRACK-2.

**Order of the switches:** never set `EGC_SCHEDULE_SYNC_WORKER` on egc-api before `EGC_GHL_TAG_OUTBOX` is on for the Hub and `EGC_GHL_TAG_DRAIN_ENABLED` is on for egc-worker. With the mirror on and the outbox off, confirmations and reminders stop.

## Owner checklist

1. Leave `EGC_OPERATIONS_CHECKIN_TASKS_ENABLED` and `EGC_OPERATIONS_INBOUND_TASKS_ENABLED` unset. HighLevel keeps the check-ins and the unanswered-text follow-ups.
2. Deploy the Hub (Cloudflare Pages) together with or before egc-api. Until the Hub change is live, a Hub with operations on still expects egc-api's platform check-in task, and a closeout would get no check-in at all.
3. The check-in task reads and creates contact tasks with the `contacts.readonly` and `contacts.write` scopes the HighLevel private integration already has ([messaging owner setup](messaging-owner-setup.md), section 2). No new scope is needed.
4. Confirm `HIGHLEVEL_API_KEY` and `HIGHLEVEL_LOCATION_ID` (or the `GHL_*` names) are set on **Cloudflare Pages**, not only on Railway. Once the bridge is on, a field completion creates its check-in from the Hub, so it needs the Pages key too. If either is missing, the completion's internal handoff shows **blocked** with "HighLevel is not configured for the Hub"; set them, then tap **Retry internal handoff** on that job. A job whose handoff shows "HighLevel refused the 6-month check-in task" needs the key's contact access or the job's linked contact checked before the retry.
5. Once the bridge is on, each verified field completion creates the HighLevel task "6-month garage check-in". Check that no HighLevel workflow triggered by **Task Added** reacts to it in a way you do not want (the [automation registry](automation-registry.md) lists this under "HighLevel Note Added / Task Added workflows").
6. Close any inbound review tasks that are still open. With `EGC_OPERATIONS_INBOUND_TASKS_ENABLED` unset, the reconciler no longer runs, including the pass that completed these tasks after staff replied, so an open one would stay open and later show as overdue on the Hub home screen and in MCP `egc.whats_overdue` and `egc.followups_due`. Such tasks exist only if the follow-up owner setting (`EGC_OPERATIONS_FOLLOWUP_POLICY_ENABLED`) was on; with it off, there are none. To check, run this read-only query on the Railway Postgres: `select id, title, due_at from tasks where dedupe_key like 'inbound_reply:%' and status in ('open','in_progress');`. Complete each one in the Hub Action Center (outcome: "Handled in HighLevel conversations"), or with MCP `actions.complete` or `actions.cancel`. Do not update the table directly: task writes must go through the operations service.
7. In the operations status (`egc.operations_status`), the `post_job_followups` row now reports new check-ins as `highlevel`: HighLevel owns them. A `pending` or `blocked` row left from before the deploy clears to `highlevel` when that job's completion handoff or closeout is retried; nothing else needs doing.
8. Turning `EGC_OPERATIONS_CHECKIN_TASKS_ENABLED` off after it was on is safe. A job that already has a platform check-in task keeps it, and the Hub adds no HighLevel task for that job. Turning it on is not recommended. If you do, turn it on only when no completion handoff or closeout sync shows error or pending. A field completion retried after the switch reads HighLevel first: if its earlier attempt did create the HighLevel task, that task stays the check-in and the job's handoff asks operations to cancel the duplicate platform task in the Action Center. A retried closeout is not checked this way.
9. In HighLevel, make sure no workflow depends on a contact being created by a messaging dry run. That no longer happens.
10. To have the Hub tell HighLevel about bookings, moves, cancellations, no-shows and walkthrough outcomes without a manager's page open, set `EGC_GHL_TAG_OUTBOX=true` on Cloudflare Pages and `EGC_GHL_TAG_DRAIN_ENABLED=true` on the Railway egc-worker (it signs with `API_BEARER_TOKEN`, like the messaging cron). Your existing booking and reminder workflows keep working unchanged: the same tags are added.
11. Never set `EGC_SCHEDULE_SYNC_WORKER=true` on egc-api before item 10. With the mirror on and the outbox off, confirmations and reminders stop.
12. Before item 10, decide whether you want HighLevel workflows on the new tags (`egc-visit-rescheduled`, `egc-visit-cancelled`, `egc-visit-no-show`, `egc-walkthrough-no-show`, `egc-walkthrough-lost`, `egc-quote-to-follow`) and on the appointment status Cancelled or No Show. None is needed; with no workflow the tags are tracking only. Check that no existing workflow on **Appointment Status** would message a customer you did not intend to.
13. While `EGC_MESSAGING_DRY_RUN` is not exactly `false`, a change for a visit whose customer has no linked HighLevel contact is retried for about 4 hours (8 attempts) and then shows as stuck. A manager's page sync, customer-resolve or adoption that links the contact in that time tells HighLevel without a Retry; a page sync after it also hands the stuck entry a fresh attempt. Otherwise link the customer's contact and press **Retry** on the Command center. Visits the Hub never mirrors to HighLevel (no contact, `not_needed`) are not told, as today, and are not counted as stuck.
14. Booking, move and restore tags are added after the visit's HighLevel appointment is written, as today, so a workflow on `egc-hub-scheduled`, `egc-walkthrough-scheduled`, `egc-job-scheduled` or `egc-reminder-Nd` that reads the appointment (its date or time, or a wait relative to it) keeps working. No workflow change is needed.
15. Cleanout and reorg visits keep today's `egc-walkthrough-scheduled` tag with the outbox on (see **Cleanouts and reorganizations** above). If you want them to start your job workflow instead, ask for that as its own change; do not edit the walkthrough workflow for it.
16. After item 10, open the Command center. If it says **The HighLevel tag worker has not run**, HighLevel is not being told about bookings, moves, cancellations or outcomes (only a manager's **Retry** tells it). Check that egc-worker is running with `EGC_GHL_TAG_DRAIN_ENABLED=true`, that `API_BEARER_TOKEN` matches between Railway and the Hub, that the Hub uses v2 service auth, and that `HIGHLEVEL_API_KEY` and `HIGHLEVEL_LOCATION_ID` are set on Cloudflare Pages. The Railway log line `ghl_tag_drain` shows the code. The message clears within 2 minutes of the worker's next completed run.
17. A cancel or no-show for a booking HighLevel never heard of (no appointment and no confirmation tags yet, for example a visit cancelled minutes after it was booked) adds no tag and changes nothing in HighLevel, so a workflow on `egc-visit-cancelled` or `egc-visit-no-show` never reaches a customer who was never confirmed.
18. Dispatch readiness (FIX-DISPATCH-READY) only reads. The card's reminder chip comes from the visit's outbox entry (with item 10) or from the page's calendar sync fields, and says which; "Reminder set" only when the entry or the page's sync (which now records the reminder tag it added, `automationReminderTag`) told HighLevel `egc-reminder-{n}d`, never after a Game Plan sync (tool=game_plan adds no reminder tag) and never from a page sync whose tags the outbox owned (it records `outbox`, read as not confirmed, also after the outbox is turned off); it writes nothing to HighLevel, and a Notify customer change on a booked visit is told with the visit's next date or time change. `EGC_DISPATCH_NOTIFY_IMPORTED_ON=true` on Cloudflare Pages (off by default) makes the first Dispatch booking of an imported Jobber job save Notify customer on, so that booking adds `egc-reminder-{n}d` and writes the appointment with HighLevel's notifications on, like any booking with Notify on. Leave it unset if imported customers should hear nothing until someone turns the toggle on per job.
