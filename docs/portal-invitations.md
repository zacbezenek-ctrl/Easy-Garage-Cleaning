# Portal links by text: the accepted-quote invitation, quote ready and job done

The Hub texts a customer the private link to their project portal in three cases. All three share one delivery path (`functions/_lib/portal-invitation.js`): HighLevel SMS to the saved customer's phone, or email when there is no valid phone; once per job per kind; the same skips and holds. Every customer-facing word is in `PORTAL_MESSAGES` in that file.

| Kind | When | Switch | Text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Accepted quote (`approved`) | A signed walkthrough's first HighLevel sync, the owner releasing held prices, or a recorded approval | none: sends today | "Hi {first}, your quote is approved. Here is your private Easy Garage Cleaning project portal for job details, messages, and payments: {link}" |
| Quote ready (`quote_ready`) | An estimator sends a quote for review (**Send to customer**, **Send saved quote** or **Mark as sent**) | "Text the portal link when a quote is sent" — **off** by default | A deposit is due: "Hi {first}, your Easy Garage Cleaning quote is ready. You can review it, approve it and pay the deposit in your private project portal: {link}". No deposit due (already paid, none required, a payment waiting for the office's review, or the figures cannot be read): "Hi {first}, your Easy Garage Cleaning quote is ready. You can review it and approve it in your private project portal: {link}" |
| Job done (`job_done`) | The crew completes the job (field completion) | "Text a fresh portal link when a job is done" — **off** by default | A balance is due: "Hi {first}, your Easy Garage Cleaning job is done. Thank you! Your remaining balance, receipts and photos are in your private project portal: {link}". Nothing due (or a payment waiting for the office's review, or the balance cannot be read): "Hi {first}, your Easy Garage Cleaning job is done. Thank you! Your receipts and job details are in your private project portal: {link}" |

The deposit and balance wording follows the money the customer's portal shows (`servedMoneyTotals` in the `MONEY_UNIFIED_TOTALS` mode). A crew cash or check receipt the office has not reviewed yet (`fieldPaymentPendingId`, `customerPaymentNeedsReview`) is not in those figures, so that customer is never told a deposit or balance is due.

"Hi {first}, your" becomes "Your" when no name is saved. The email fallbacks say the same in one paragraph with an **Open your Easy Garage Cleaning project portal** button (subjects: "Your Easy Garage Cleaning project portal", "Your Easy Garage Cleaning quote is ready", "Your Easy Garage Cleaning job is done"). The texts use only GSM-7 characters (no em or en dashes, curly quotes, ellipses or bullets): one character outside that set makes the whole text a Unicode (UCS-2) SMS of about twice the segments. With a 30-day link each is a plain SMS of 3 segments (4 with a very long first name), where the earlier em dash made the job-done texts 7 Unicode segments; `tests/portal-sms-gsm7.test.mjs` checks every variant. The emails keep their typography ("job is done — thank you!"). `docs/automation-registry.md` lists all of them with their exact templates.

## The owner's switches

- The quote-ready and job-done switches are `portalLinkQuoteReady` and `portalLinkJobDone` in the server-only `messaging_settings/automation` record (beside `paused`); both default to `false`. While a switch is off nothing of that kind is sent, and turning it on later never texts quotes sent or jobs finished before.
- **Turning a switch off ends the waits.** A text of that kind still waiting (for 8 AM, the pause, a retry) is closed on its next retry as `off` (reason `owner_switch_off`), so the retries stop and **Estimates & payments** says "not sent · switched off". Turning a switch on records when (`portalLinkQuoteReadyOnAt`, `portalLinkJobDoneOnAt`); a text still waiting from before that moment is closed the same way and never sent.
- **Only a new trigger starts an ended text again.** A text that ended unsent (`off`, `not_eligible`, `suppressed`, `contact_mismatch`) starts again as a new request only from its own trigger: the estimator sending the quote again, or the crew completing the job again (`fresh` in `sendPortalLink`, passed by `functions/api/quote-draft.js` and `functions/api/field-jobs.js`). The automatic retries (the Hub's page load, the messaging cron) and a manager's click never restart one: they choose jobs from the display copy `portalLinks.<kind>`, which a concurrent job write can leave saying it waits after the ledger ended, so they mirror the ended ledger onto the job again (the copy heals, the retries stop) and send nothing. A copy that already matches is not rewritten.
- **HighLevel may text at the same moment.** The quote send adds the `egc-estimate-ready` tag, and the crew's completion adds `egc-job-complete` and `egc-review-ready` (`docs/automation-registry.md`: `hub.quote_send_estimate_ready`, `ghl.lifecycle.estimate_ready`, `ghl.closeout_workflows`). Before turning a switch on, check the HighLevel workflows on those tags and turn them off or change them, so the customer does not get two texts. The switch screen says this next to each switch.
- Hub → **Message templates** → **Portal link texts** shows each switch with a one-line description and the exact text it sends. Only business users (Zac, Tyler, Alex) can turn them on or off (`POST /api/message-templates {action:'set_portal_link', requestId, link, enabled}`, idempotent per request, written under the record's revision; an invalid record is never written into).
- A record that cannot be interpreted holds every automatic message (fail closed), these two included.

## Rules for every kind

- Delivery uses the saved customer's phone via HighLevel SMS, or email when there is no valid phone number. The linked contact's identity and location must match the saved job.
- Job notifications off, HighLevel DND, a business-account project (the ledger records `suppressed` / `business_account_job` before any HighLevel call; this includes a visit Dispatch created under a shared business project, which has no `businessAccountId` of its own: the account root its link would open, `customerAccountOwnerJobId` verified hop by hop, is checked before HighLevel, again before the link is minted and once more after the claim), held prices (`pricesPendingApproval`) and a closed job are never texted. For the accepted-quote text closed means cancelled, no-show, completed, invoiced, paid, review requested, closed or superseded; for quote ready the quote must still be sent, not approved and not expired (`estimate.validUntil` on or after today's Denver date when it goes out: a quote sent on its last evening and held overnight is closed `not_eligible` / `quote_expired`, because the portal would refuse its approval); for job done the job must be completed (or later) and not cancelled, no-show or superseded.
- **Job done also waits for the crew's follow-up.** A completion the crew marked "follow-up required" (missing materials, damage, a return visit: `fieldExecution.completion.hasIssues` or an open `fieldExecution.attention`) is not texted; the ledger records `not_eligible` / `follow_up_open` and the Hub says "crew follow-up is open". A later completion without an open follow-up sends it. A job-done text also never goes out more than 48 hours after the completion (`job_done_too_long_ago`), however long quiet hours, the pause or a switch held it.
- A link added while the contact is being checked still records the suppression before any message is claimed, and the job is re-read after the claim: a business link, a price hold or a notification opt-out saved in between releases the claim, and a job that cannot be re-read releases it as a retryable `storage_unavailable`, before HighLevel is called. The company's members open a business project from the Business Hub (see `docs/business-client-hub.md`).
- The private link lasts 30 days (job done mints a fresh one). The portal session lasts seven days. Tokens are never saved in staff-readable job documents or returned from any send endpoint.
- **Once per job.** A conditional storage claim limits sending to one message per job per kind: `portal_invitations/{jobId}` for the accepted-quote text, `portal_link_sends/{kind}~{jobId}` for the others (both server-only). A reopened and re-completed job, a resent quote revision, a repeated save or a concurrent request never texts again.
- The job carries a display copy only: `customerPortalInvitation` and `portalLinks.<kind>` (kind, status, channel, masked recipient, times; never the link). **Estimates & payments** shows each, for example "Portal link texted 10/21 2:14 PM (quote ready) · (•••) •••-0123".
- `submitted` means HighLevel accepted the message; it does not prove carrier or inbox delivery. Check the HighLevel conversation for final delivery. A definite rejection can be retried. A timeout, server error or lost delivery-status write is marked uncertain and never automatically resent. Check HighLevel before manually sharing a fresh link.
- The accepted-quote row on **Estimates & payments** shows when and where it went, like the others: "Portal text queued in HighLevel 10/21 2:14 PM · (•••) •••-0123".
- **Quiet hours.** Every automatic send follows the project's rule for automatic customer messages: only between 08:00 and 20:00 Denver time (`QUIET_HOURS` in `functions/_lib/message-policies.js`). Outside it the ledger records `deferred` with reason `quiet_hours` and `notBefore` (the next 8:00 AM), spends no attempt, and the next automatic retry after that time sends it.
- **Messaging pause.** While `messaging_settings/automation` has `paused: true` (or cannot be interpreted), automatic sends record `deferred` with reason `messaging_paused` (or `messaging_settings_invalid`) and wait. If the record cannot be read at all, nothing is sent or recorded.
- **Automatic retries.** Held and definitely failed messages of every kind are retried when a manager loads or refreshes the Hub (three per load, ten minutes apart, five attempts) while server messaging (`EGC_SERVER_MESSAGING_ENABLED`) is off; once it is on, the signed messaging cron runs the same retries instead. A held message therefore waits for one of those after 8 AM, and the Hub's label says which: "· sends on the next manager Hub load" or "· the server sends it automatically".
- Only a manager's own **Retry portal delivery** / **Send portal now** / **Retry portal link** click (`manual: true`) sends during quiet hours or the pause. `EGC_MESSAGING_ENABLED` and `EGC_MESSAGING_DRY_RUN` (the quote builder's "Customer messaging is turned off") do not govern these texts; removing `HIGHLEVEL_API_KEY`, setting the pause, turning job notifications off, or (for quote ready and job done) the owner's switch are what stop them.

## The accepted-quote invitation, from the server, right away

- **Signed walkthroughs.** Right after the signed save and its photo uploads, the walkthrough page of a quote author without business access (a Sales estimator) asks the server to run the walkthrough's first HighLevel sync (`POST /api/walkthrough-handoff {action:'sync', requestId, jobId}` → `syncSavedHandoff` in `functions/api/highlevel.js`): the contact, the pinned internal brief note, the job appointment when a calendar is configured, the existing tags and stage, then the invitation under the rules above. It runs in a request of its own, after the photos, so it never shares the save's Cloudflare subrequest budget and never writes the job while the page uploads photos (each photo upload carries the job's revision). Only the job's current signed request can be synced this way, by a quote author who may open the job. The job records the page's sync result (`syncStatus` `synced`, or `error` with the page's backoff), and the page says what happened; when another sync holds the job it follows that one (`GET /api/walkthrough-handoff?view=sync&jobId=`). A retry of the save after that sync finished ("Retry remaining synchronization", "Saved — verify again") is offered no second sync; the page reads `view=sync` and keeps saying what the sync and the invitation did, never that a manager still has to. The photo uploads work for a Sales estimator too: the quote author who signed the job's current handoff (`acceptance.recordedBy`) may attach that walkthrough's reference photos (`POST /api/field-jobs` action `photo`, category `walkthrough` only) to the job without being on its crew, and reads back only the job's revision and its reference photos (`functions/api/field-jobs.js` `handoffSigner`, `referenceDetail`); everything else on the job stays with the assigned crew and managers. The signer may do so only before field work starts (the handoff's own `work_started` rule: a stage before dispatch — scheduled, unscheduled, draft, confirmed or crew assigned — no crew action recorded and no field record but reference photos); after that the answer is 403 `FIELD_REFERENCE_PHOTO_WORK_STARTED` and a manager attaches any more. A job keeps at most 40 reference photos (`WALKTHROUGH_PHOTO_LIMIT`, whoever attaches them: 409 `FIELD_WALKTHROUGH_PHOTO_LIMIT`), so the crew's before, after and damage photos always keep 60 of the job's 100 places and a large walkthrough can never stop the job from being completed. The walkthrough page treats either answer as final: it says how many photos stay on the iPad instead of asking for another retry. A manager's iPad keeps running the sync itself through `/api/highlevel`, as before. The save itself starts nothing in the background. If the page closes before it asks, a manager's Hub load finishes the sync, as before.
- **One sync at a time.** Whoever starts a signed handoff's sync (the server at a quote author's request, the iPad of a business user, a manager's Hub retry) claims it on the job (`handoffSyncClaim`, a compare-and-set on the job's revision) and releases it when done; a claim older than ten minutes is abandoned and may be taken over. A sync that finds the handoff already synced for its request and current appointment request (`handoffSyncedRequestId`, `handoffSyncedKey`) replays the saved outcome (and the invitation's settled state) instead of writing to HighLevel again, so whichever runs second sees it synced. A page request that finds another sync running waits for it (up to 15 seconds), then replays it or answers `HANDOFF_SYNC_IN_PROGRESS` ("an interrupted sync clears within 10 minutes"); the Hub retries it after its backoff. Notes and appointments are also idempotent per request on the operations platform.
- **Held prices.** A signed job held for the owner's price approval gets no invitation at its sync. When the owner releases the prices (`prices.release`, or a menu approval or settings save that now covers the job), the server sends the pending invitation after the response; a menu approval sends at most three this way and leaves the rest to the automatic retries.
- **Recorded approvals.** `POST /api/money estimate.record_approval` hands the invitation to the server after the response, as well as the page's own HighLevel lifecycle call (the ledger keeps it to one).
- **Covered by a quote-ready link.** If a quote-ready link was delivered (`submitted`), or its send is unconfirmed (`uncertain`, or still `sending` two minutes after it started: HighLevel may have delivered it, and an ambiguous send is never followed by a second text automatically), it still has at least three of its 30 days to run, and it still opens (the link version and account root it was minted under, `linkVersion` and `linkRoot` on its ledger, are still the account's: no staff revocation and no move under another account since), the accepted-quote text is not sent: the ledger records `covered` (reason `quote_ready_link_delivered`; `coveredBy.unconfirmed` for an unconfirmed one) and the Hub shows "Portal invitation not needed · quote-ready link texted … still works" or "Portal invitation not sent · quote-ready link … unconfirmed · check HighLevel". A quote-ready text still being sent makes the invitation wait for the next automatic retry (`quote_ready_in_flight`). If no quote-ready link was delivered (switch off, DND, failed), it is about to expire, or staff revoked the account's links since it went, the invitation goes as before, with a link that works. A revocation after the approval was recorded `covered` is like one after a sent invitation: staff share a fresh link with **Copy customer portal**. A quote-ready text whose quote was approved (or expired) while it was being sent is released before HighLevel is called.
- **Subrequests and time.** The page's sync request has its own Cloudflare subrequest budget (50 per request on the Free plan, 1000 on Workers Paid); a full sync with the invitation makes about 40 calls, so on the Free plan it fits with little room, and moving to Workers Paid (GO-LIVE B2) removes the limit. It also runs under `waitUntil`, which keeps it going for up to 30 seconds after a closed page; each HighLevel call allows 15 seconds and the appointment sync 45. A sync cut short leaves its claim until it lapses (ten minutes): until then the Hub's retry answers `HANDOFF_SYNC_IN_PROGRESS` and tries again after its backoff, and every step is idempotent per request.

## Estimators copy the link

- On their own quote's send screen (after **Send**), or the saved-quote view, an estimator can **Copy portal link**: the same 30-day link as the office's **Copy customer portal** (`POST /api/customer-portal-link`).
- Only the estimator who owns the quote may: its author (`createdBy`, or the quote draft's `createdBy`) or the estimator of its source walkthrough (the visit's crew lead or assigned person, or who started the visit). Anyone else gets 403 `PORTAL_LINK_NOT_YOURS`; a business project, or a visit whose account root is one, never gets a homeowner link (409, nothing recorded).
- Every copy is recorded on the job (`portalLinkCopies`: who, when, estimator or office; the newest 20). An estimator's copy that cannot be recorded creates no link. Business users keep their button unchanged (their copies are recorded too, best effort).

## Required production configuration

Existing Cloudflare Pages production secrets must include:

- `HUB_SESSION_SECRET`: dedicated random staff/session signing secret (also signs portal links unless `CUSTOMER_PORTAL_SECRET` is provided).
- `HUB_AUTH_USERS_JSON`: configured staff accounts with generated PBKDF2 password hashes. This is required for authorized Hub operations; do not restore legacy embedded credentials.
- `FIREBASE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON`: credentials for project `egcw-1ec83`, with the Firestore access needed to read jobs and write delivery metadata. Keep the JSON encrypted in Cloudflare, outside the repository.
- `HIGHLEVEL_API_KEY` and `HIGHLEVEL_LOCATION_ID`: contact read/upsert and conversation-message write access; an operational SMS/email sender in the existing account.

The full owner checklist for customer messaging (HighLevel scopes, A2P 10DLC, sender number and email domain, workflows to adjust per message kind, the inbound reply webhook, template approval and a dry-run acceptance pass) is in [Customer messaging owner setup](messaging-owner-setup.md).

Publish the reviewed Firestore rules from this repository as part of the secure portal setup. Configure secrets in Cloudflare and redeploy before using the feature. The invitation code does not fall back to anonymous Firestore access.

This page does not record whether production has these secrets today. (On September 6, 2026 the Cloudflare production settings had the HighLevel connection but not `HUB_SESSION_SECRET`, `HUB_AUTH_USERS_JSON` or `FIREBASE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON`.) Before relying on automatic invitations, confirm that staff can sign in to the Hub (which needs the first two) and that **Hub → Integrations** shows `FIREBASE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON`, `HIGHLEVEL_API_KEY` and `HIGHLEVEL_LOCATION_ID` as ready.

## Revoking portal links

An owner or manager can end every homeowner portal link and portal session for a customer account without sending anything to the customer.

- `GET /api/customer-portal-revoke?jobId=<job>` returns the verified account job, its current link version, its revision and `collaboratorCount` (active authorized people saved on the account).
- `POST /api/customer-portal-revoke` with `{ "requestId": "<uuid>", "jobId": "<job>", "expectedRevision": "<revision from GET>", "clearCollaborators": true }` increments `customerPortalLinkVersion` on the account job. Every field must be a JSON string except `clearCollaborators`, which must be a boolean when present; anything else returns 400 `CUSTOMER_PORTAL_REVOKE_INVALID_REQUEST`. A stale revision returns 409; retrying the same `requestId` returns the saved result without a second increment. Receipts are kept in the server-only `customerPortalOperations` collection (Firestore rules deny all client access).
- `clearCollaborators` defaults to `true` when omitted. A Hub control for this endpoint should omit it or send `true` unless staff explicitly choose to keep the saved people. In the same atomic commit as the version increment, it empties the account job's saved authorized people (`customerCollaborators`). Collaborator invitations and sessions are rechecked against that list on every request, so they stop working immediately. This matters because anyone holding a leaked owner link could add themselves as an authorized person and mint an invitation before the revocation. The response and receipt record only `removedCollaboratorCount`, never names or contacts. Send `false` only when you are sure the saved people are legitimate. Invitations that carry a link version still end, but older invitations without one keep working.
- Links and sessions created before this feature carry no version and keep working until the account's first revocation. New links from **Copy customer portal** and accepted-quote invitations carry the current version (`lv`) and the verified account job id (`lr`), so they work until the next revocation. Exchanging any link for a portal session binds the session the same way.
- Minting an owner (homeowner) access or session token without an explicit link version throws a programmer error. A token without one would count as version 0 and lock the owner out after the account's first revocation, so every caller must pass `customerPortalLinkVersion` of the verified account job (`readCustomerPortalContext(...).linkVersion`, or `customerPortalLinkAccount(...)` in staff code).
- A token that names an account job (`lr`) is rejected with `CUSTOMER_PORTAL_ACCESS_REVOKED` once its job resolves to a different account job, so moving a job under another account never revives a revoked link. Tokens without `lr` keep the earlier behavior.
- A recurring or linked visit uses its verified account job, so one revocation covers every job in that account.
- Business-account project access is managed from the business account and is not affected.
- After revoking, create a fresh link with **Copy customer portal** and share it only with the customer. The customer can re-add their authorized people from the fresh link.
- Collaborator invitations from `create_collaborator_invite` carry the account's current link version (`lv`) and account job id (`lr`), so a revocation ends them even when `clearCollaborators` is `false`. `save_collaborators` writes with the account job's observed revision, so a save that loaded the account before a revocation committed returns 409 and cannot re-add the cleared people.
