How pricing works

Single item or full garage — we quote flat

Unlike hourly haulers, we set one complete price after seeing the property with you. These ranges help you plan; the exact number is approved before work begins.

Single item

$99–150

One couch, mattress, appliance, hot tub, treadmill, or bulky piece. Priced individually — not by the hour.

Partial load

Full garage / estate
