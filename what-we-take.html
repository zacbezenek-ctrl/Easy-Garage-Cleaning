What we haul

We take most garage & household items

We take

  • Furniture — couches, beds, dressers, tables
  • Appliances — fridges, washers, dryers, water heaters
  • Mattresses & box springs
  • Exercise equipment — treadmills, bikes, weights
  • Hot tubs & spas (dismantled on site)
  • Yard debris & outdoor furniture
  • Boxes, bins, tools, and general clutter
  • Storage unit contents

We can't take

  • Paint, chemicals & solvents
  • Asbestos or hazardous materials
  • Oil drums & fuel tanks
  • Medical or biohazard waste
