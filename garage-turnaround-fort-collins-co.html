Turnaround day

The one-day outline, hour by hour

Every garage is different, but a typical two-car turnaround in Fort Collins follows the same arc. Here's how the day runs when the crew pulls up in the morning:

8:00 am — Walkthrough and sort plan. We walk the garage with you and agree on three categories: keep, donate, haul. You make the calls — we never decide on our own what counts as trash. This takes about twenty to thirty minutes, and it's the only part of the day that truly needs you there.

8:30 am — The full pull-out. Everything comes out onto the driveway into sorted staging zones. Seeing it all in daylight makes the keep pile honest; most homeowners trim it further once the duplicates and rusted-out items are side by side.

10:30 am — Donation and disposal runs. While part of the crew preps the walls, the truck starts its runs: usable items to local donation centers, the rest to the transfer station. Junk hauling, dump fees, and donation drop-offs are already inside your flat-rate quote — there's no separate junk bill.

12:30 pm — Shelving and wall systems. With the garage empty, we install the shelving and wall storage from the layout we agreed on at quote time: heavy-duty shelving units, hooks for bikes and ladders, and overhead racks where the ceiling allows. Getting things off the floor is what protects the parking space long-term.

2:30 pm — Zones, labels, and re-homing. Keep-items go back in by category — tools, camping, sports, holiday, yard care — each with a labeled home. Labels matter more than they sound: they're how the rest of the household puts things back without asking.

4:00 pm — Parking layout, sweep, and deep clean. We sweep the slab, knock down cobwebs, spot-clean the floor, and mark a clear parking footprint so storage never creeps back into the bay.

5:00 pm — Final walkthrough. You inspect the space, we hand over your donation receipt, and you park inside that night.

After day one

The stay-clean system: why it doesn't relapse

A garage transformation only counts if it holds. The reason most garages refill isn't laziness — it's that nothing had a designated home, so every item that entered the garage landed on the floor. The turnaround ends with a simple system designed to prevent exactly that:

Zones beat memory. When camping gear, tools, and holiday bins each have a labeled shelf, putting things away takes seconds and requires no decisions. Anyone in the household can maintain it, because the labels do the thinking.

The floor stays empty by rule. The marked parking layout turns the bay into protected space. The rule we leave you with is blunt: if it's on the floor inside the lines, it's in the wrong place. Vertical storage on walls and overhead racks holds everything else.

One in, one out. New bike? Old one goes to the donation bin. We set up a single staging tote near the door for outgoing donations, so giving things away becomes a habit instead of a project.

A fifteen-minute seasonal reset. The checklist pairs a quick tidy with the seasons — swap snow shovels and ice melt forward in fall, push them back and bring camping gear forward in spring. Fifteen minutes a season keeps the system current instead of letting it drift.

If life happens anyway — a move, a renovation, a garage full of someone else's boxes — re-organize visits are available on request, and they're quick when the shelving and zones are already in place. FAQ

Questions we get a lot

Q.01 What is a garage turnaround?

A garage turnaround is a one-day garage transformation: full cleanout, donation runs, keep-items re-homed on wall shelving, labeled zones, a marked parking layout, and a sweep and deep clean — all in a single visit.

Q.02 How much does a garage turnaround cost in Fort Collins?

Most one-day turnarounds run $1,200–$2,200 flat-rate. Lighter clear-outs start around $400–$650, packed two-car cleanouts run $650–$1,200, and whole-property resets top out at $2,200–$3,500.

Q.03 Can you really finish in one day?

Yes for most one- and two-car garages — hauling, donation runs, and shelving install happen in parallel. Extremely packed garages occasionally need a second morning, and we tell you that up front from your photos.

Q.04 Is junk hauling included?

Yes. Hauling, dump fees, and donation drop-offs are built into the flat-rate turnaround quote — there is no separate junk removal bill.

Q.05 Do I need to be home all day?

No. Most homeowners walk the garage with us at the start to confirm keep, donate, and haul piles, then we handle the rest and do a final walkthrough together.

Q.06 What happens to donated items?

Usable items go to local donation centers like the Habitat for Humanity ReStore or ARC, and we hand you the donation receipt for your records.

Q.07 Do you supply shelving and bins?

We plan the layout and install basic shelving and bins you purchase. We don't markup products — installation labor is built into the flat rate.

Q.08 Will my car actually fit inside afterward?

That's the point of the parking layout — we plan wall and overhead storage around your vehicle's footprint and leave a clear, marked parking zone.

Q.09 How is a turnaround different from a regular cleanout?

A cleanout removes junk and sweeps. A turnaround adds shelving, labeled zones, a parking layout, and a deep clean so the garage comes back fully functional — not just empty.

Q.10 How do I get a turnaround quote?

Text wide photos of your garage to (970) 999-1818 or call. We respond within 5 minutes with a flat-rate quote, and you only pay after approving it.
