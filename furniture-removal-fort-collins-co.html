FAQ

Questions we get a lot

Q.01How much does furniture removal cost in Fort Collins?

Single pieces typically run $99–$150. Multiple items or a garage full of furniture falls in the $250–$650 range depending on volume.

Q.02Do you remove furniture from upstairs?

Ground floor and garage yes. Second-floor removals depend on access — send photos and we'll quote accurately.

Q.03Will you donate my old furniture?

If it's in usable condition, yes — Habitat ReStore, ARC, or Goodwill. You get the donation receipt.

Q.04Do you disassemble furniture?

Basic disassembly (bed frames, tables) when needed for safe removal. Complex built-ins may require a carpenter.

Q.05Can you remove a couch from my garage?

Yes — garage furniture removal is one of our most common calls in Fort Collins.

Q.06Is there a surcharge for heavy items?

No per-item surcharges. Weight is factored into your flat-rate quote upfront.

Q.07Do I need to wrap or bag furniture?

No prep needed. We protect floors and walls during carry-out.

Q.08How fast can you pick up furniture?

Same-day and next-day often available. Text photos for fastest scheduling.

Q.09What if my furniture is moldy or broken?

We still haul it — it goes to disposal instead of donation. No judgment, just removal.

Q.10Do you remove office furniture from home garages?

Yes — desks, filing cabinets, and chairs from home offices and garages.
