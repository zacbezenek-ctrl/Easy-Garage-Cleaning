FAQ

Questions about reviews

Q.01 Where should I leave my review?

Google is the most helpful place. We'll text you a direct link after your job. You can also find us by searching "Easy Garage Cleaning Fort Collins" on Google Maps.

Q.02 What should I mention in my review?

Whatever is most useful to someone in your situation. The service you used (garage cleanout, junk removal, appliance pickup, etc.), how scheduling and pricing worked, and whether you'd recommend us. Honest detail helps more than star ratings alone.

Q.03 Do you offer discounts for reviews?

No. We never offer incentives, discounts, or freebies in exchange for reviews. That violates Google's policies and undermines trust. We earn reviews by doing good work.

Q.04 When will I get the review link?

We send it via text message after your job is complete — typically the same day. If you don't receive it, text us at (970) 999-1818 and we'll resend.

Q.05 Can I leave a review if I only got a quote?

Google reviews should reflect actual service experiences. If you received a quote but didn't book, we appreciate the interest but ask that reviews come from completed jobs.
