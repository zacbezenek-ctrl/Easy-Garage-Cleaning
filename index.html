FAQ

Questions we get a lot

What is a garage cleanout? A garage cleanout is a full haul-out of unwanted items from your garage — furniture, boxes, appliances, and clutter — followed by donation of usable goods and a floor sweep so you can park inside again. Easy Garage Cleaning quotes flat-rate from photos with no hourly billing.
Q.01How much does a garage cleanout cost in Fort Collins?

Most jobs fall into four ranges: single-item pickup ($99–$150), small loads ($250–$400), medium garage cleanouts ($400–$650), and large or full garages ($650+). Text photos for your exact flat-rate quote — no hourly billing.

Q.02How do I get a quote with photos?

Text photos to (970) 999-1818 or upload them in our quote form. We respond within 5 minutes during business hours with a firm flat-rate price.

Q.03What do you haul away?

Furniture, appliances, mattresses, boxes, tools, yard debris, storage unit contents, and general household junk. We cannot haul hazardous materials like liquid paint, asbestos, or medical waste.

Q.04Do you donate usable items?

Yes. Working appliances, furniture, and household goods go to Habitat ReStore, ARC Thrift, and Goodwill. You receive a donation receipt in your name.

Q.05How fast can you come out?

We respond within 5 minutes and offer same-day service when our schedule allows. Most jobs book within 2–3 days of the initial quote.

Q.06Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Easy Garage Cleaning LLC is a Colorado-registered LLC with general liability and commercial auto insurance.

Q.07Do I need to be home during the cleanout?

You need to be present for the initial 15–20 minute walkthrough. After that, many clients leave us to finish the job.

Q.08Do you charge by the hour or flat rate?

Flat rate only. The price we quote before the job is the price you pay — no clock-watching, no line-item surprises.

Q.09What is the No-Surprise Quote Guarantee?

If the scope matches what you described, your quoted price is locked. We will not add fees at the end. If something unexpected comes up, we discuss it with you before hauling.

Q.10What areas of Northern Colorado do you serve?

Fort Collins, Loveland, Windsor, Timnath, Wellington, Severance, and LaPorte — including Old Town, Fossil Creek, Centerra, and surrounding neighborhoods.
