Before we get started

A clearer plan.
Fewer unknowns.

More details in our frequently asked questions.

Do I need to empty the garage first?

No. The walkthrough is a chance to see the space as it is. Point out anything you want to keep, valuables and items that need special care. We agree on sorting and removal responsibilities in the plan.

How do you price a garage project?

We review the amount of material, access, cleaning and any storage work at a free on-site walkthrough. You approve one flat-rate scope before work begins. See the pricing guide.

What is the No-Surprise Quote Guarantee?

If the job matches the scope approved during the walkthrough, your quoted price is locked. No hourly add-ons, no "we found more than expected" fees at the end. If something unexpected comes up, we talk to you first.

Can you just pick up a few items?

Yes. Start with our junk removal service. If you can stage a small load outside, check the separate curbside pickup option and its current terms.

What happens to the items you remove?

We identify reusable items that may be accepted by local donation partners and plan disposal for the rest. Condition and acceptance rules matter. Check accepted items and exceptions.
