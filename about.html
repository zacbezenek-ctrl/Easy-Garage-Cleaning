MEET ZAC

How a neighbor’s garage started a business

Zac Bezenek grew up in Fort Collins, went to Poudre High School, and is now studying business at CSU. He started Easy Garage Cleaning LLC after helping a neighbor clear out a garage that had accumulated forty years of life — tools, furniture, appliances, boxes that hadn't been opened since the 90s.

The job took a weekend. His neighbor was relieved. And Zac kept thinking about all the people who needed exactly that kind of help but couldn't find a service that felt trustworthy. Most of what he found was impersonal franchise crews: rotating staff, opaque pricing, add-on fees for appliances and heavy items. The kind of companies where you don't know who's showing up until they ring the doorbell.

That bothered him. So he built something different.

Every job is flat-rate. Zac hates the anxiety of not knowing what you'll owe at the end — the way hourly billing turns you into a clock-watcher while people haul your stuff. So the price is set before anyone shows up, and that's the price you pay.

Zac is on every single job. He works alongside Tyler, his two-person crew. When you book Easy Garage Cleaning, you know exactly who's coming.

DONE BY DINNER GUARANTEE

If your job isn’t finished by end of day, you pay nothing. Zac stands behind this because it’s easy to when you show up prepared and work until it’s done.

FLAT-RATE, ALWAYS

DONATE FIRST, HAUL SECOND

HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. No surprises.

Every job runs the same way. Simple by design, because complicated processes are where trust breaks down.

STEP 01

You call or fill out the form

Tell us what you’ve got — or send a few photos. We give you a firm flat-rate number, usually within minutes. No vague ranges, no "it depends." A number you can say yes or no to, right then.

STEP 02

Zac and Tyler show up

We do a 15-minute walkthrough with you — you point out what’s staying and what’s going. Then we get to work. You can stay and supervise or run an errand. Either way, we’ll check in if anything’s unclear before it leaves the property.

STEP 03

Done by dinner, receipt in hand

Donable items go to Habitat ReStore, ARC Thrift, or Goodwill. Everything else goes to the landfill or recycling. We hand you an itemized donation receipt and collect payment at job completion. Garage is clear. Day is done.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

These aren’t policies. They’re just how we work.

Zac wrote these down not as marketing language but because customers kept asking why he does things a certain way. Here’s the honest answer.

Flat-rate, always

Hourly billing puts the wrong incentives on the table. We give you one number before we start and hold to it no matter how long the job takes. You shouldn’t be watching the clock while we haul your stuff.

The owner is on every job

When Zac started this business, he decided he’d be on every job — not manage from an office while dispatch sends a crew. That’s still true. His name is on the truck. He’s accountable in a way that a franchise crew member isn’t.

Donate first, haul second

Usable items shouldn’t go to the landfill if Habitat ReStore or ARC will take them. We sort as we work and make the donation run ourselves. You get the tax receipt. The community gets the items. Nobody has to make a special trip.

No hidden fees, ever

No per-appliance surcharges. No dump fee line items added after the fact. No "that item was heavier than we expected" upcharges. The quote we give you is the bill you receive.

Done by Dinner or it’s free

If your job isn’t finished by end of day, you pay nothing. Zac made this guarantee because it forces him to show up prepared, staff the job correctly, and work without dragging it out. It also means customers don’t have to wonder whether the job will be finished today.
